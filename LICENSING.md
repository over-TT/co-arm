# Licensing status

No open-source, open-hardware, documentation, CAD, or media license has been
selected for this repository yet. Unless and until explicit license files are
added, no permission to copy, modify, manufacture from, or redistribute this
material is granted beyond rights that apply automatically under law.

The repository owner still needs to choose:

- a software license for firmware, gateway, MCP, and dashboard code;
- a hardware/CAD license for designs and manufacturing files;
- a documentation license;
- a media license or explicit all-rights-reserved notice;
- the public attribution name.

Third-party material keeps its original license and must be documented before
it is added. Do not infer that vendor names, links, or compatible hardware make
vendor CAD or firmware redistributable.
