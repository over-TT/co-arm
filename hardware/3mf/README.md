# 3D-printed parts

These are the parts from my current arm.

## Parts

- `co-arm_base_main-body_r01.3mf` — Base with the large gear built in.
- `co-arm_base_servo-gear_r01.3mf` — Small gear for the Base servo.
- `co-arm_base_bearing-bottom_r01.3mf` — Sits underneath the large Base bearing.
- `co-arm_base_shaft-base_r01.3mf` — Sits on top of the bearing.
- `co-arm_base_level-2-platform_r01.3mf` — Bolts to the Shaft Base and carries the lower-arm servo.
- `co-arm_shoulder_servo-mount_r01.3mf` — Mount for the lower-arm servo.
- `co-arm_shoulder_lower-link_r01.3mf` — Lower arm link.
- `co-arm_elbow_upper-link_r01.3mf` — Upper arm link.
- `co-arm_elbow_servo-cover_r01.3mf` — Cover for the top servo.
- `co-arm_camera_servo-mount_r01.3mf` — Holds the camera servo at the end of the arm.
- `co-arm_camera_holder_r01.3mf` — Holds the camera.
- `co-arm_camera_cover_r01.3mf` — Closes the camera holder.

## Build order

1. Put the bearing-bottom piece in the Base.
2. Fit the large bearing above it.
3. Put the Shaft Base on the bearing.
4. Fit the lower-arm servo and its mount before bolting on the Level 2 platform.
5. Mesh the small servo gear with the large Base gear.
6. Add the lower link, upper link, and top-servo cover.
7. Fit the camera-servo mount, camera holder, camera, and camera cover.
